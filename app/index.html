Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10

One playthrough, zero online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no mission replay, free-roam after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving. Method: PowerPyx roadmap; counts: official descriptions.

  1. Phase 1 — Story first (60–80 hours). Play the story and do the one thing that cannot wait: in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house — the only possibly missable trophy. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave) and one before the final mission. Scan vehicles and study animals with binoculars as you go: 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of 255 animals count toward 100%.
  2. Phase 2 — Stranger strands as they unlock. Andy (Lucia, Chapter 3), Kenton (Chapter 1), Billy (after Coffee Break), Randy (Chapter 3) and Tyrone (Jason, Chapter 3) each end in a story trophy, and 8 stranger strands earn Strangers & Freaks. All of them stay available after the story.
  3. Phase 3 — Activities & collectibles during and after the story. Shooting-range golds (42 challenges), drone races (14 locations), WAiNK scans (100), card sets (3), meditations (18 spots), races (20 wins), challenges (50 of 80).
  4. Phase 4 — Cleanup + 100% checklist, then Platinum. After the story the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: any 100 optional missions, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations. Finish it and A Long Road pops — then the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, follows.

Missable trophies: exactly one

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason’s house is gone forever after Chapter 1, and PowerPyx says it is still unconfirmed whether it is strictly required for the trophy — do it anyway, in the morning after Down to Earth, by talking to Lucia in the kitchen and following her to the balcony. Every other trophy is safe: story trophies pop automatically, stranger strands stay open after the story, and the 100% checklist can be finished in free-roam. The save discipline is what protects you: a manual save at the start of each chapter, plus one before the final mission.
